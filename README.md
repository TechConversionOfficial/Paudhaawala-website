## About the business

Paudhaawala presents itself as a plant retailer and green-living specialist serving homes, offices and other spaces. The public website organises plants by suitability and use, including **indoor**, **outdoor**, **low-maintenance**, **air-purifying**, **Vastu & good-luck** and **office** categories. It also sells self-watering planters and selected bundled plant collections.

Alongside consumer plant shopping, the website offers broader greening services. These include gardening guidance, plant maintenance, corporate office solutions, landscaping for villas and farmhouses, and bulk plant supply. This means the platform has to serve both **individual buyers choosing a houseplant** and **organisations enquiring about larger green spaces**.

The site's public contact section lists its location in Mohali, Punjab, support email **contact@paudhaawala.com** and telephone **+91 98141 52020**. Product availability, delivery claims, discounts and customer testimonials are subject to change and remain the company's published information.

## The problem

Buying a living plant online involves questions that do not arise in most conventional ecommerce stores. A customer needs to assess plant size, suitable light, watering requirements, pot options, suitability for the intended room and the practicalities of delivery. A beginner may not even know which species to search for.

An effective plant-shopping website therefore needs to make discovery intuitive. Rather than relying on botanical names alone, it should help visitors **shop by room, level of care and purpose**, explain product options and provide guidance after purchase. At the same time, commercial clients need a clear enquiry route for landscaping and bulk orders rather than a regular consumer cart.

These are the challenges suggested by the live site's offering. No independent audit of a previous Paudhaawala website or its historic conversion figures was available.

## How the website presents the experience

The points below describe **features visible on the public website**, rather than undocumented internal development decisions.

1. **Start with familiar shopping categories.** Customers can browse indoor and outdoor plants, low-maintenance varieties, plants promoted for air purification, and options suited to different rooms or workspaces.
2. **Help people find plants without knowing their names.** The [Shop](https://paudhaawala.com/shop/) includes product search, sorting, filters and use-case labels. These offer alternatives to a simple list of species.
3. **Show useful choices on product pages.** Indexed product pages show plant details and variant controls for size, pot type and colour, with an add-to-cart action and, on some pages, a delivery-pincode checker.
4. **Give self-watering planters prominence.** A dedicated homepage section introduces their purpose and benefits for customers who cannot water plants frequently.
5. **Explain the delivery journey.** The homepage presents a simple sequence: browse and choose, pack and ship, then unbox and grow. This addresses customers' concerns about buying live plants for delivery.
6. **Include practical aftercare advice.** The [Plant Care](https://paudhaawala.com/plant-care/) section covers watering, light and placement, repotting, feeding and general plant health.
7. **Create a distinct commercial route.** The homepage introduces office greening, landscaping, luxury villas, hospitality spaces and wholesale plant supply. The [Landscape](https://paudhaawala.com/landscape/) page goes deeper into these enquiries.
8. **Make browsing social and visual.** The site uses product photography, featured collections, reviews and customer testimonials. Any success, satisfaction or health-related claim should be evaluated against its underlying evidence.

## Standards the website should be reviewed against

These are **appropriate evaluation references**, not claims that a formal audit or certification has already been completed.

- **[Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals):** test image-rich product pages and mobile catalogue layouts for loading, responsiveness and layout stability.
- **[WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/):** check accessible filters, variant selectors, form labels, keyboard navigation, visible focus and meaningful plant-image descriptions.
- **[Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials):** review useful category explanations, discoverable products, descriptive titles, internal links and crawlable content.
- **[Schema.org](https://schema.org/):** evaluate accurate Product, Offer, Organisation and BreadcrumbList structured data where applicable.
- **Customer information and payments:** review applicable privacy requirements, payment-provider handling and secure treatment of delivery addresses and orders; cart controls alone do not establish checkout security.
- **Delivery and living-product accuracy:** clearly state delivery coverage, plant-size variation, replacement/refund policies and live-plant care expectations.
- **Claims and product data:** review descriptions such as “air-purifying”, pet-safe, medicinal or other wellness-related benefits against reliable evidence and appropriate qualifications.
